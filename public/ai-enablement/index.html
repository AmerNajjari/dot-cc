Questions

Before you book.

Do I need any technical background?

No. The session starts from the work you already do. If you can write an email, you can do everything we cover.

Which AI tools does the session use?

The ones that fit your work. We pick them in the first hour, based on your role and your tasks, so you leave with a short list you will actually use rather than a tour of every tool.

Can the session be in Arabic?

Yes. The session can run in Arabic or English, and we show how the tools work in both languages.

Is it one to one or for a team?

The four-hour session is one to one. For a whole team there is a two-day plan, and for a company that wants AI agents doing real work every day, we build its own system.
