Brand story

Two strands.
One truth.

Every customer leaves two traces: what they say, and what happened to them. Helixion reads both.

Look at the X in the name. It is two bars with a cross between them. The top bar is what the customer said. The bottom bar is what happened on their account: the order, the payment, the refund. The cross is where Helixion holds the two side by side.

Most tools read only the words. Words lie, by accident or on purpose: in Saudi dialect, "Mashallah, you didn't fall short" can be warm thanks or a sharp complaint. The account tells you which. When the two strands agree, the message goes out. When they disagree, Helixion holds it and hands it to a person.

The name comes from the helix, the shape of DNA: two strands that twist around each other and never let go. The site shows it as a living double helix. The product is that shape doing a job.

01Said.
The message, read in its dialect.
02Happened.
The account's events, in order.
03Compared.
Where the two agree, or do not.
04Decided.
By the rules, with a reason, logged.
Tagline
ما نخليك تهلوس
Always set in Arabic, on Arabic and English pages alike. Meaning, for reference: "We won't let you hallucinate." AI reads words and makes things up; Helixion checks the words against the account, so neither you nor your AI acts on a story that is not true.
Positioning line
Helixion reads every message against the account, and holds marketing when the two disagree.
How to write the name
HELIXION in the logo only. Helixion in running text, in both languages. In Arabic: هيليكسيون to confirm.
Never
Helexion, HeliXion, Helix, Helixion AI, or the X typed as a letter.

The physician
who reads the chart.

Reads before speaking. Never prescribes on one symptom. Hands you to a person when unsure.

Tone of voice

Clear, warm,
and on your side.

We talk like a sharp colleague who has your back. Never like a legal notice, never like a robot.

01

Lead with the why

"We held the promo for Customer B: their card was charged and no order came through." The reason is the message.

02

Talk like a person

Short sentences, everyday words, contractions welcome. If you wouldn't say it out loud, rewrite it.

03

The AI reads, rules decide

We never say "the AI decided". It gives a reading, a rule makes the call, and we say which one.

04

Real numbers, real units

186.50 SAR, 14:41, 3 minutes. Exact is friendly. Vague is not.

05

Arabic, the way people speak it

Customers are quoted in their own dialect. Interface Arabic is simple and warm, never stiff.

06

Hand it over gladly

"A teammate will take it from here" is a feature, not an apology.

Voice in action

How we sound when...

...a promotion is held

Reason first, then the next step.

Heads up: we held the weekend promo for Customer B. Their card was charged with no order. Give them a call first?

...we can't read a message

Say so. Nothing goes out without a reading.

We couldn't read this one, so nothing went out. Can you take a look?

...a client asks how accurate it is

No percentages. Show the real cases.

Let us show you instead: here are 11 real runs and the rule behind each call.

...we meet an investor

Relaxed, but the numbers come from the one canon, as ranges.

Here is the plan, the risks, and the numbers behind each one.

...an agent overrides a hold

Trust the person; keep the record.

Done, the hold is lifted. We've noted your reason in the log.

...we got it wrong

Own it, fix it, say what changed.

That one's on us: we held a message that should have gone. The rule is fixed in v1.1.

...we write for a client's customers

The client's voice, not ours. Helixion never signs a customer message.

The X

Two bars 1u thick and 4.5u wide. Between them a cross 2.2u square, with 0.4u of air above and below. The cross is the rung.

Clear space

2u on every side: about two letter stems. Nothing enters the dashed box, not even the edge of a photo.

Minimum size

Wordmark: 96 px wide on screen, 24 mm in print. The X alone: 16 px, 5 mm. Below that, use the X.

Page10

The motif

The Helix.

The DNA from the site, given a job. It is the product, drawn, and it is always slowly turning.

What the customer saidWhat happened on the accountThe rung where they disagree
Strands
Cyan is the reading, violet the account. Never swapped, never a third strand.
Rungs
Every quarter period, violet at 30%. One amber rung at most, and only where a real disagreement is shown.
Scale
Period 48 px and amplitude 8 px at 1x. Scale both together, never one alone.
Once
One Helix per screen or page. Never a border, a bullet or a background pattern.
Motion
The Helix turns: both strands drift through each other once every 4 seconds, at a steady speed. Rungs stay in place and stretch as the strands cross. The amber rung never moves. It stops for viewers who ask for less motion.
Arabic
The curve is symmetric, so it never mirrors. Only its reading direction starts from the right.

Logo motion

The strands meet.

The two bars slide in from either side, the cross turns into place between them, then the letters arrive.

0 msTop bar from the start edge, bottom bar from the end edge.
380 msThe cross turns 90 degrees into place: the rung.
700 msThe letters rise 8 px and fade in.
1300 msStill. Nothing loops, nothing bounces.
One curve
Everything eases out on the same curve. No bounce, no spin, no elastic.
Durations
140 ms for hovers, 240 ms for panels, 480 ms for bars and reveals, 1300 ms for the logo.
Arabic
The bars enter from the right first. The logo itself never mirrors.
Reduced motion
If the viewer asks for less motion, the logo simply appears.

Laws and artifact matrix

Seven laws.

One verb per colour

Violet acts, glow points, cyan reads, amber holds, red stops, green sends.

The model suggests, the rules decide

Cyan never sits on a button, a decision or a check mark.

No reason, no chip

Every status shows its rule and its plain-words reason within one glance.

Evidence before action

The reading and the account first, the button last.

Group with space first

Space, then shared colour, then a shared surface. A border only when all three fail.

Numbers are exact

Space Grotesk, tabular figures, unit attached. Sample figures say so.

One curve, one glow

One easing everywhere. One glowing element per screen: the primary action.
