The market problem in one line: Saudi brands can’t hear their customers. The firms we serve, small and mid-sized online stores, delivery, retail and services, talk to customers on WhatsApp and sell to them with campaigns, and two problems sit between those jobs. How it decides

AI suggests. Rules decide.

1 · Message

Arrives on WhatsApp or SMS, in Saudi dialect.

2 · Reading

The AI gives odds: positive, sarcastic, complaint.

3 · Account check

The Dissonance Engine compares it with orders, payments and tickets.

4 · Governor

Fixed rules decide: send, hold, or route to a person.

Money first

An unresolved payment problem holds all marketing, whatever the AI says.

No reading, no send

If the AI fails or refuses, nothing goes out and a person reviews.

Every decision a row

What decided, which rule fired and on what inputs. Ready for any audit.

Helixion · 08

Four steps. The message arrives. The AI reads it and gives probabilities only; it takes no action. The Dissonance Engine checks it against the account. Then the governor, plain code, decides. Three rules matter most. Money first: a failed payment or a charge with no order holds all marketing, and that rule reads no AI output at all, so a wrong AI reading can never release it. No reading, no send. And every decision is a row in our database: who or what decided, which rule fired, and on what inputs. That row is the audit trail.
